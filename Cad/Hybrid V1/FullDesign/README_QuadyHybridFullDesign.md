## Confirmed design inputs

- Four hip centers use the existing 150 x 100 mm spacing.
- Eight STS-3215 12 V servos are represented: hip and knee on each leg.
- Two batteries are each 139 x 47 x 40 mm.
- Each battery has its own four-servo Waveshare adapter reservation.
- The preferred controller is the supplied ESP32-S3 board; the Pi 3B is only an underside reservation.
- The ground datum is intentionally below the preserved foot envelope, with the target pose encoded from the current 53.882 mm X/Z hip-to-knee vector.

## Print guidance

- Use PETG-CF or PA12 nylon for the structural printed parts.
- Use at least four perimeter walls and 35-45% gyroid infill.
- Print the flat cassette plate and upper link in their shown flat orientations where practical.
- Keep at least 5 mm of solid material around cassette holes; use washers under the board.
- Do not use the battery or servo proxy bodies as print geometry.

## Alignment feedback loop

The `65_REFERENCE_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT` section is intentionally visible. Use it
as a review gate after every geometry change:

1. Fit the whole assembly in isometric view and confirm all four printed upper links and all eight servo proxies are present.
2. Switch to top view and confirm the four hip axes form the 150 x 100 mm rectangle and each cassette is centered on its axis.
3. Switch to front view and confirm each hip axis, servo shaft, upper-link boss, knee axis, and knee servo shaft are coaxial.
4. Switch to side view and confirm each thin hip-to-knee centerline terminates on both axis datums.
5. Confirm each visible preserved-foot envelope begins at its knee axis; hide the audit group only after this visual check.
6. If any view fails, change the master transform/pose, rerun the generator, and repeat the checks before printing.
